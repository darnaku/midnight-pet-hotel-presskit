The game in 30 seconds

Midnight Pet Hotel is a cozy management game about running a hotel for pets from sundown to sunrise.

Check in sleepy cats, energetic puppies, picky penguins and plenty of other guests, find each of them a room that suits them, and keep everyone fed, rested and entertained until morning.

Over 25 nights, a tiny three-room hotel grows into a twelve-room business with its own night crew, all building up to the Grand Midnight Gala.

What you do

  • Find every guest the right room. Each room shows Good fit, Okay or Poor fit, with the reason: a noisy neighbour, a roommate who won't get along, a penguin with no igloo bed.
  • Keep them happy until morning. Bowls run empty, rooms get messy and boredom sets in. Guests pay at checkout for their whole stay, and good stays bring tips, reviews and more guests.
  • Furnish and grow. Arrange furniture room by room, add upgrades, and renovate the East Wing to go from three rooms to twelve.
  • Hire a night crew. Five staff to clean, cook, play and groom. You decide where they work first.
  • 25 nights in five chapters. Thunderstorms, a late-night rush, a celebrity guest, an adoption fair and a very thorough health inspector, ending at the Grand Midnight Gala.
  • Play your way. Cozy Mode for a relaxed game, Management Mode for more of a challenge, and Endless Mode after the finale.

How it was made

Midnight Pet Hotel was made by Darnaku with a lot of help from AI tools. Here's who did what.

Darnaku

Came up with the game and its ideas, set the direction, tested it throughout, gave the feedback that shaped it, and approved each change and the final release.

Claude Code

Anthropic's AI coding assistant was used extensively for the programming and implementation, working from Darnaku's direction and feedback. The logo, trailer edit and store images were also put together this way, from real gameplay captures.

Music

The original soundtrack, ten pieces and four jingles, was produced by Darnaku with ElevenLabs Music, then edited and looped for the game.

Art and sound effects

The 3D pets, furniture, interface and sound effects come from Kenney's free CC0 asset packs. The pet furniture, icons and app icon were made for this game. Built with Godot Engine.
